The long western and southwestern feeder course is not individually named; nearby Boyd wording does not establish its exact name partition. Both fragments carry modeled mainstem roles, which do not make every part Clarence. Coastal and feeder-to-main-river transitions remain unassigned at reach level. The mapped Hunter River at Greta station and direct Hunter line label support Hunter River as a representative system identity. Figure 4.46 separately labels Goulburn and Munmurra along the western corridor, so this modeled mainstem is not uniformly Hunter. These are regional channel associations, not exact source-reach assignments. Feeder junctions, storage-related boundaries and the coastal ending remain unassigned at reach level; the northeastern Glenbawn/Pages branch is not the selected upper path.
