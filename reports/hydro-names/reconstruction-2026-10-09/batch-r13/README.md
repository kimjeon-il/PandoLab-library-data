Three new, bounded actual-inventory investigations against immutable Pando baseline `fd6744f5e72a0c1a107452dbde6d416ab57237db`. The live deployment was not checked. These are new research records, not restored historical verdicts.
- **1159110463: Calcasieu Lake.** The full narrow northern reach, widening eastern basin, southern end and western lower lobe broadly correspond to the NOAA Calcasieu Lake overview. The northern cap crosses finer upstream detail; the long western boundary generalizes ship-channel and marsh margins. The blue project outline is not the lake boundary. Category: `supported_generalized_water_identity`.
- **1159107033: Lake Maurepas.** The complete rounded ring corresponds to the mapped Lake Maurepas immediately west of Lake Pontchartrain. Its broad southern curve, western side and narrower north/northeast extent were considered. Fine west-bank indentation and channels are unresolved at this scale; the target does not reach the separate Pontchartrain basin. Category: `supported_generalized_water_identity`.
- **1159110475: Lake Salvador.** The whole silhouette is visible on the acquired CPRA map: a northern lobe, narrowed connection, broad southern basin and southwestern extension. Lake Salvador is readable over the southern basin. The unlabelled northern lobe and fine connections prevent applying that name uniformly to the whole polygon. This is a completed bounded scope assessment with follow-up, not a demonstrated multi-name union. Category: `whole_polygon_scope_hold`.
