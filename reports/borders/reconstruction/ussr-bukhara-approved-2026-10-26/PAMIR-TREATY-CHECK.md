# Pamir 20.343 km attachment: limited treaty check, 2026-10-26

Scope: approved endpoint72.95308143E37.28876141N to current-outline intersection73.13037624E37.38270091N, along modern Tajik–Afghan Pamir River reference. No new map search or geometry change.

## Conclusion
The examined primary treaty texts and official US boundary study do not identify a territorial exchange relocating this particular20km corridor. A positive historical basis exists for a river boundary here: IBS26(1983), PDF/page12, attributes the river boundary Eshkashem–Lake Sari-Qul to1869–73 Anglo-Russian agreements. This supports treating the atlas discrepancy as a cartographic-location/generalization problem for the chosen display model, rather than inventing an unverified cession. It does NOT certify every modern coordinate as unchanged since1922 or establish the exact physical reason for the atlas displacement.

## Spatial and legal distinctions
- 1926 Urta-Tugai: a return/recognition episode involving a lower Pyandzh island near the Kyzylsu confluence, not the Pamir River at73E. Iranica's Boundaries iii describes this episode and location; its exact February date is not used as a runtime date here. The1946 treaty explicitly says the island continues to belong to Afghanistan.
- 1946 Frontier Agreement, UNTS31 printed158–160, articles1–3: thalweg/main-fairway/median rules and island allocation. Article2 entrusts Khamiab–Lake Sar-i-Kul delimitation to a commission, so the scope reaches the Pamir corridor, although Article1 names Amu-Darya/Pyandzh. It is unsafe to claim this entire agreement made no positional or island-allocation changes. It contains no cession specification for our20km segment.
- 1948: demarcation documents dated29September1948 are incorporated by1958 article1. This was boundary surveying/definition, not evidence by itself of a particular land exchange.
- 1958 Frontier Regime Treaty, UNTS321 printed166–168 article1: Zulfikar–Zor-Kul follows1946/1948 instruments; east of Zor-Kul follows1895 protocols. The list includes separate western re-demarcation and Khamiab–Zor-Kul demarcation documents. No new20km exchange is stated.
- 1895 and1981 eastern Pamir: IBS26 page12 places these eastward from Lake Zorkul towards the Chinese tripoint. Their existence is not evidence that the present20km, west of the lake, changed.
